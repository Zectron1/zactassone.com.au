About

Here's Zac.

IT business owner, content creator, networker. Based in Sydney's Hills District.

I'm Zac Tassone, Managing Director of Zectron Web & IT Services. I started Zectron in 2012 and built it into a managed IT, cybersecurity, Microsoft 365, hosting and web business that looks after small and medium businesses all over Sydney.

Most business owners I meet don't want to become IT experts. They want their tech to work, their data to be safe and someone they can call who explains things properly. That's what I've built Zectron around.

Zac the IT Guy

Zac the IT Guy is where I share the things clients ask me every week, in a minute or less: quick fixes, scam warnings and the stuff nobody explains properly. New videos go up every Tuesday and Thursday on Instagram, YouTube and TikTok.

About

Here's Zac.

I'm the IT guy who'd rather explain it properly than baffle you with jargon. I run a business, I make videos, I'm all in on BNI, and I don't do boring.

I started Zectron in 2012, a year out of school, to challenge the overpriced, underdelivered IT I kept seeing. I built it into a managed IT, cybersecurity, Microsoft 365, hosting and web business looking after small and medium businesses all over Sydney, from our office in Norwest.

Most business owners I meet don't want to become IT experts. They want their tech to work, their data to be safe and someone they can call who explains things properly. That's what I've built Zectron around, and it's the whole idea behind Zac the IT Guy: no jargon, no scare tactics, just what you need to know and what to do next.

Networking changed my business, and now I love helping other owners grow the same way. In 2026 I was named BNI Ambassador of the Year at the BNI Australia National Conference, which still makes me grin.

60-Second Fix

Why your Wi-Fi is slow

The quick checks that fix most home and office Wi-Fi problems.

Read the quick version
  • Restart the router and modem, in that order, and give them two minutes.
  • Move the router out of cupboards and away from the floor, TVs and fish tanks.
  • Check how many devices are connected. Old phones and smart plugs add up.
  • Use the 5 GHz network when you are close to the router, 2.4 GHz when you are further away.
  • If it is still slow on a cable, the problem is your internet plan, not your Wi-Fi.
60-Second Fix

Speed up a slow computer

What to do before you spend money on a new one.

Read the quick version
  • Restart it properly. Most slow computers have not been restarted in weeks.
  • Check what starts automatically and turn off anything you don't need.
  • Make sure you have at least 15 to 20 percent free disk space.
  • Install pending updates, then restart again.
  • If it still has a spinning hard drive, an SSD upgrade is often cheaper than a new machine.
Things Clients Ask Me

Cloud storage is not a backup

Why OneDrive and Dropbox alone won't save you.

Read the quick version
  • Sync tools copy your mistakes too: delete a file and it disappears everywhere.
  • Ransomware can encrypt synced files just as easily as local ones.
  • Version history has limits on how far back it goes.
  • A real backup is a separate copy, kept somewhere else, that you have tested restoring.
  • For a business, Microsoft 365 needs its own backup. Microsoft says so in its own service agreement.
Red Flag Friday

Spot a fake invoice email

The signs that give a scam invoice away.

Read the quick version
  • Bank details have "changed" and the email says it is urgent.
  • The sender address is close to right but not quite.
  • The invoice is for something you don't recognise, or it is a supplier you pay but the wording feels off.
  • Always confirm new bank details with a phone call to a number you already have, never one in the email.
  • If in doubt, forward it to your IT person before you pay anything.
